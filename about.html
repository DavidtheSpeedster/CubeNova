About CubeNova

A free speedcubing timer and trainer that works offline.

CubeNova is a timer and practice tool for speedcubers, made by David the Speedster. It is free, runs in your browser, and after one visit it works with no connection. You can install it on your phone or computer like an app.

Open the timer

What it does

  • Timer: WCA-style scrambles for 3×3 up to 7×7, 2×2, Pyraminx, Skewb, Megaminx and Square-1, with inspection, penalties and sessions.
  • Stats: best, ao5, ao12, mean, graphs, goals and a coach that suggests what to practise from your own solves.
  • Ways to enter a time: hold the spacebar or the screen, type a time in, or solve on the built-in virtual cube.
  • Learn tab: step-by-step guides, plus trainers with a picture for every case in OLL, PLL, F2L, ZBLL, and the 2×2 sets (Ortega OLL and PBL, CLL, EG).
  • Online battle: race friends on the same scramble in a shared room.
  • Your data: solves stay on your device, and you can export a full backup at any time.

Experimental features

Support for a Stackmat timer, a GAN timer and smart cubes is experimental. It has not been tested on every device, so treat it as a beta.
