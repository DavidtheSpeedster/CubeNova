About CubeNova v47
A free speedcubing timer. Privacy: your solves, settings and background image are stored only in this browser on this device. There are no accounts, no tracking and nothing is uploaded; the only outside request is for the page fonts, which are saved for offline use. Use the backup export in Settings to move your data between devices. Scrambles are generated on your device and follow WCA-style formats.
